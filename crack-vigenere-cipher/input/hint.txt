VIGENERE CIPHER CHALLENGE
=========================

You have intercepted an encrypted message. Your mission is to decrypt it and recover the hidden flag.

Hints:
- The encryption algorithm is the classic Vigenere cipher.
- The key is a common 6-letter English word closely related to the world of cryptography.
- Only alphabetic characters have been shifted; spaces, punctuation, braces, and underscores are unchanged.
- The flag format is FLAG{...} and appears somewhere in the plaintext.

Tools that may help:
- Frequency analysis
- Kasiski examination
- Index of coincidence

Good luck!
