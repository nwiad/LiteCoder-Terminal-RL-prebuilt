The city council approved a new budget for public transportation. Bus routes will be expanded to cover underserved neighborhoods. The mayor praised the decision as a step toward equity.

Funding for the project comes from a combination of federal grants and local taxes. Construction of new bus stops is expected to begin next quarter. Residents have expressed strong support for the initiative.

Critics argue that the budget does not allocate enough for maintenance of existing infrastructure. Several council members called for a review of current spending priorities. The debate is expected to continue at the next council meeting.
